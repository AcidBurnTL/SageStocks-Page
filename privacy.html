Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 10, 2026

SageStocks ("we", "our", or "the App") is operated by Florin. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive:

  • Your Apple-assigned user identifier (a unique, app-scoped ID)
  • Your display name (only if you choose to share it with Apple Sign-In)

We do not collect your email address, phone number, or Apple ID password.

Usage Data. We store data you create within the App:

  • Stock categories you follow
  • Stocks you add to your watchlist (up to 50)
  • Your viewing history of predictions (to display your history within the App)

Device Information. If you enable push notifications, we store your device token to send notifications. We do not collect device identifiers for tracking purposes.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your email address
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, or other device data
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not collect financial account information, brokerage data, or trading activity

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: Your Apple user identifier is used to create and maintain your account
  • App Functionality: Your watchlist and category preferences are stored to provide personalized content
  • Push Notifications: Device tokens are used solely to deliver notifications you have opted into
  • Service Improvement: Aggregated, non-identifying usage patterns may be used to improve the App

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. Authentication tokens on your device are stored in the iOS Keychain, the most secure storage available on iOS. All communication between the App and our servers uses HTTPS encryption.

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted database connections, JWT-based authentication with short-lived access tokens, and automatic token rotation.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is not shared with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

We may disclose information only if required by law, such as in response to a valid legal process.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Aggregated, non-identifying data (such as prediction accuracy statistics) may be retained indefinitely.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored by the App
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent by signing out and deleting your account

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

SageStocks is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect information from children. The App contains financial market data and AI-generated predictions that are intended for adult users.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
